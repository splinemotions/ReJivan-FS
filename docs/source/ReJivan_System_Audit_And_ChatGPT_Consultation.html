1. Executive Overview & Core Mission

ReJivan ("A Personal Prajñā Nurse for Every Family") is an affordable remote patient monitoring and emergency response system engineered for dual deployment across in-home elderly care and hospital "Virtual Ward" rooms where medical staff cannot maintain continuous physical bedside presence.

The system is anchored in the Andaman & Nicobar Islands (UT)—a geographically fragmented territory comprising 36 inhabited islands with severe maritime logistics constraints and referral bottlenecks centered at GB Pant Hospital, Port Blair. ReJivan couples continuous wearable vital monitoring with privacy-preserving computer vision sentinels to deliver instantaneous deterioration triage and emergency intervention without transmitting or storing private raw video.

2. Detailed Working Breakdown: Properly Implemented Features

The following nine core functional subsystems have been rigorously engineered, validated, and deployed across the web platform (prototype/) and offline-capable Android client (app-android/):

2.1 Clinical Remote Patient Monitoring (RPM) Physiological Drift Engine

  • Working Mechanism: Generates continuous, physiologically realistic bio-telemetry sampled every 1.5 seconds. Incorporates mathematically modeled respiratory sinus arrhythmia (pulse jitter ~1.2s beat-to-beat), blood pressure baroreflex drift, and micro-variations in SpO2 and body temperature.
  • Multi-Condition Profiles: Patient-specific profiles for Hypertension (Anita Sharma, 67F), COPD / Post-Surgical Recovery (Ram Prakash, 71M), and Pediatric Asthma.
  • Real-Time Historical Telemetry Buffers: Dynamic FIFO queues (20 data points) drive live animating SVG trend sparklines for Heart Rate, SpO2, Blood Pressure, and Core Temperature without client-side layout shifts.
  • Interactive Clinical Scenario Stress-Testing: Web dashboard controls allow evaluators to inject acute episodes (🟢 Baseline, ⚠️ BP Crisis 172/106, 🚨 Hypoxemia 89%, 📉 Bradycardia 50 bpm) to verify instantaneous triage reactivity.

2.2 Clinical Rules Engine & Deterioration Scoring (NEWS2 / MEWS)

  • Working Mechanism: A deterministic, rule-based clinical scoring engine evaluates all incoming vital parameters against UK Royal College of Physicians (RCP) NEWS2 and MEWS clinical guidelines.
  • Dual Safety Envelopes: Segregates thresholds into Caution (e.g., HR 100–119 bpm, Sys BP 140–159 mmHg, SpO2 93–94%) and Danger / Critical (e.g., HR ≥ 120 or ≤ 45 bpm, Sys BP ≥ 160 or ≤ 85 mmHg, SpO2 ≤ 92%).
  • Consecutive-Reading Verification & Anti-Flapping: Implemented in reliability.js to require 2+ consecutive dangerous telemetry cycles before triggering emergency escalation, filtering out transient sensor detachments or measurement artifacts.

2.3 Automated Multi-Tier Emergency Escalation Ladder

  • Working Mechanism: The moment a confirmed CRITICAL / DANGER condition occurs (vital collapse or camera-detected floor fall), an autonomous escalation state machine engages immediately:
    1. Tier 1 — Primary Family Caregiver: Direct call initiation (0–4.2s response window).
    2. Tier 2 — Secondary Backup Contact: Automated escalation if Tier 1 fails or is unanswered within 5.5s (5.5–9.8s).
    3. Tier 3 — Emergency Dispatch (108/112): Automatic ambulance and paramedic dispatch packet generated with localized GPS coordinates, patient clinical history, and live vitals snapshot (11.0–13.2s).
  • Dashboard Visibility: The Alerts tab displays the complete live call-flow ladder, caller IDs, contact status, and event timestamps in real time.

2.4 Medication Administration Record (MAR) & Adherence Engine

  • Working Mechanism: Manages scheduled clinical dosing regimens organized into Morning, Afternoon, Evening, and Bedtime slots.
  • Interactive Administration: Provides one-tap "Take Dose" verification that updates medication status, computes adherence percentages, and logs an immutable timestamped record.
  • Offline Persistence: Syncs to local storage and Android SharedPreferences with automatic background reconciliation when network access is restored.

2.5 Hospital "Virtual Ward" Central Nurse Station

  • Working Mechanism: Dedicated institutional view simulating the central nursing console at GB Pant Hospital, Port Blair.
  • Dynamic Multi-Bed Triage Queue: Sorts inpatient beds (Bed 301, 302, 303, ICU-A) by physiological risk priority (Critical Red > Caution Yellow > Stable Green).
  • Clinical Export & Rapid Incident Drills: Enables duty nurses to inspect active telemetry snapshots, acknowledge active alarms, and export clinical audit logs in structured JSON format.

2.7 DPDP Act 2023 Privacy-First Architecture & Hardware Lifecycle

  • On-Demand Hardware Camera Engagement: Physical camera hardware is NEVER opened at startup. The webcam remains completely unpowered (LED indicator strictly OFF). Hardware only initializes when an authorized stream connects.
  • Instant Disengagement: When the stream pauses, terminates, or the user navigates away, cap.release() is immediately executed, cutting sensor power and turning the camera LED off.
  • DPDP Privacy Radar Mode: Evaluators can toggle Privacy Radar with one click. Raw video pixels are completely suppressed; the display renders a dark clinical radar grid showing only the 17-point glowing skeleton wireframe and kinematic metrics. Zero raw video is ever saved, recorded, or transmitted to any server.

2.8 Hospital Bed-Fall Clinical Demo Video & Automated Alarm Latching

  • Photorealistic Clinical Synthesis: Synthesized a continuous 15-second hospital surveillance video (patient_bed_fall_demo.mp4) maintaining 100% camera angle, ward geometry, and lighting consistency across 4 stages:
    • t=0–4s: Patient supine under blankets → 🟢 SAFE | Supine Resting in Bed ($V_y = 0.00\text{ m/s}$, Torso: $61^\circ$).
    • t=4–7.5s: Patient waking and sitting at edge → 🟢 SAFE | Upright Bed-Edge Sitting ($V_y = +0.08\text{ m/s}$, Torso: $15^\circ$).
    • t=7.5–9s: Slipping off mattress perimeter → 🟡 CAUTION | Sudden Bed-Exit Motion ($V_y = -0.95\text{ m/s}$, Torso: $42^\circ$).
    • t ≥ 9s: Patient collapsed horizontally on floor → 🔴 HIGH_RISK | Acute Fall / Horizontal Floor Contact ($V_y = -1.62\text{ m/s}$, Torso: $79^\circ$).
  • Emergency Interlock: Crossing the 9s threshold instantly triggers the red flashing alarm banner, sounds the emergency audio buzzer, and opens the 30-second Resident Verification Modal with automatic upright restoration cancellation.

  • Two-Way Synchronization: Features an OkHttp REST client (Sync.kt) that performs bidirectional synchronization with https://rejivan2.vercel.app when online.

3. Comprehensive List of Current Issues, Limitations & Open Challenges

In accordance with rigorous academic and engineering integrity standards, the following table lists the current architectural boundaries, hardware constraints, and open limitations of the ReJivan prototype:

# Subsystem Area Current Limitation / Open Issue Underlying Technical Cause & Impact
1 Physical Wearable Sensor Hardware Vital signs (HR, BP, SpO2, Temp, Glucose) are generated by mathematical models rather than streaming from physical Bluetooth LE (BLE) medical hardware. Physical FDA/CDSCO BLE medical devices (e.g., SanketLife ECG, Omron Evolv) require custom proprietary GATT service UUID decoders and physical hardware pairings. The prototype currently simulates these heartbeats via medical-devices.js.
2 Carrier Telecom & VoIP Dispatch The 3-tier emergency call ladder (Family → Backup → 108/112) is fully functional in logic, but actual telephony dialing is simulated on-screen. Connecting to real cellular telephone networks requires paid telecom voice API trunking (Twilio Voice, Exotel, or Tata Tele SIP trunks). For the competition prototype, call placement and retries run in real time with simulated audio/network hooks.
3 Serverless Cloud State Persistence Dynamic user actions on Vercel (registering new custom patients or adding custom medicines) reset when serverless cloud lambdas recycle. Vercel serverless functions are ephemeral and stateless. The prototype currently uses local JSON file stores (which work on desktop) and clock-derived deterministic functions on cloud. True cross-instance persistence requires a hosted database (e.g., Neon Postgres or Upstash Redis).
4 Single-Camera 2D Occlusion & Low Light Standard optical camera vision cannot track skeletal keypoints under heavy winter blankets or in pitch-black unlit rooms. RGB convolutional pose estimators (YOLO/MediaPipe) require visible human contours. Total occlusion or zero lux breaks 2D keypoint estimation. A production clinical system requires bimodal fusion with infrared/thermal sensors or ultra-wideband (UWB) radar.
5 Mobile Android Native Camera Parity The Android native app (app-android/) currently displays a simulated canvas camera feed rather than running live on-device CameraX + MLKit/YOLO. Embedding real-time YOLO11-Pose or MediaPipe Tasks inside Android requires NDK C++ bindings and on-device GPU acceleration (TFLite/LiteRT/NNAPI). The web app currently possesses the more advanced dual-vision implementation.
6 Competition Deliverables (Due 15 Oct 2026) Final submission assets (3–5 min demonstration video, 6–7 slide presentation deck, and institutional AISHE sign-off) are pending completion. Requires recording a structured video walkthrough demonstrating the live Vercel dashboard, mobile app sync, and bed-fall video alarm trigger, followed by institutional college endorsement.
