1. Executive Summary & Geographic Imperative (Andaman & Nicobar Islands)

ReJivan is an intelligent, dual-layer healthcare safety platform designed to bridge the severe medical accessibility gap across underserved and geographically isolated regions. The system combines Remote Patient Monitoring (RPM) via medically-approved wearable devices with an edge-computed, privacy-first computer vision sentinel that monitors vulnerable patients both at home and in hospital Virtual Ward settings.

The "Hack Local" Context: Andaman & Nicobar Healthcare Reality
The Andaman & Nicobar archipelago consists of 36 inhabited islands separated by vast stretches of open sea. The entire union territory relies on a single tertiary referral facility: GB Pant Hospital in Port Blair. Outer-island Primary Health Centres (PHCs) and Community Health Centres (such as Hut Bay on Little Andaman, Car Nicobar, and Diglipur) face critical specialist shortages. Evacuating a deteriorating elderly resident requires scheduled passenger ferries (taking 6 to 14 hours) or rare emergency helicopter sorties. ReJivan transforms ordinary homes into monitored clinical beds and empowers island PHCs with Virtual Ward capabilities, preventing catastrophic deteriorations before evacuation becomes impossible.

2. Complete Architecture Overview: Dual-Sentinel Protection Model

ReJivan solves the critical flaw of traditional single-channel monitors by unifying two completely independent clinical sensing modalities:

Layer 1: Continuous Bio-Telemetry (RPM)

  • Continuous physiological monitoring: Heart Rate (HR), Oxygen Saturation (SpO2), Blood Pressure (BP), Temperature, and Blood Glucose.
  • Deterministic NEWS2 / MEWS clinical deterioration score calculation.
  • Natural 1.5s physiological drift engine with respiratory sinus arrhythmia and arterial baroreflex modeling.
  • Fleet catalog of 11 FDA/CDSCO/CE certified medical wearables (e.g. SanketLife 12-lead ECG, Omron BP, FreeStyle Libre 3).

Layer 2: Edge Vision & Spatial Kinematics

  • Ultra-low latency Ultralytics YOLO-Pose on local hardware (auto-detected NVIDIA CUDA GPU) tracking 17 COCO skeletal joints.
  • DPDP Act 2023 Compliance: Zero video recording or off-site cloud streaming; physical camera LED turns OFF when monitoring is paused.
  • Universal in-browser optical differencing fallback with multi-frame descent corroboration and floor-level gating.
  • Bidirectional frame bridge (/api/yolo/process_frame) for seamless browser skeleton overlays.

4. Exhaustive Breakdown of All Added Features

Over intensive development iterations, ReJivan has evolved from an initial concept prototype into an enterprise-grade medical and computer-vision safety system. Below is the comprehensive catalog of all implemented capabilities:

A. Clinical Remote Patient Monitoring (RPM) & Bio-Telemetry Suite

  • Real-Time Dynamic Physiological Drift Engine: Unlike static demos that freeze numbers, ReJivan features a continuous 1.5-second sampling engine that models realistic biological oscillations: respiratory sinus arrhythmia (pulse rhythmically accelerating and decelerating with breath), arterial baroreflex blood pressure variations, and capillary oxygen saturation micro-variations.
  • Dynamic Animated SVG Trend Sparklines: The primary dashboard features real-time shifting historical FIFO buffers for HR, SpO2, Systolic/Diastolic BP, Temperature, and Glucose, visibly undulating on every beat without full-page re-renders.
  • Interactive Clinical Triage Scenario Injector: Evaluators can instantly stress-test the system's reactivity via one-click clinical scenarios: Baseline Stable, Hypertensive Crisis (172/106 mmHg), Acute Hypoxemia (89% SpO2), and Severe Bradycardia (50 bpm).
  • NEWS2 / MEWS Deterioration Rules Engine: Deterministic scoring adhering to the UK Royal College of Physicians standard. Multi-parameter physiological boundary filters reject impossible sensor glitches (e.g., HR > 250 bpm or SpO2 < 50%), preventing false emergency dispatch.
  • Medication Administration Record (MAR): Interactive checklist managing patient prescriptions across Morning, Afternoon, Evening, and Night slots, featuring two-way synchronization between web and Android devices.
  • Fleet Medical Device Catalogue & Connectivity Registry: Full hardware profiles for 11 clinically validated devices (FDA 510(k), CDSCO, and CE certified), including India-manufactured innovations like the SanketLife 12-Lead ECG (Agatsa Pune, <₹5,000). Per-patient device registries simulate BLE heartbeats, battery percentages, and signal quality metrics.

B. Advanced Computer Vision & Edge AI Capabilities

  • NVIDIA CUDA Hardware Acceleration: Deployed Ultralytics YOLO-Pose locally via PyTorch CUDA. The sentinel achieves 25–30 FPS real-time pose extraction with low latency (<28ms inference) and ultra-lightweight GPU memory consumption (~184 MB VRAM).
  • 17 COCO Skeletal Joint Kinematics: Continuously extracts coordinates for nose, eyes, ears, shoulders, elbows, wrists, hips, knees, and ankles. Real-time mathematical estimators calculate upper-torso inclination angle, center-of-mass descent velocity ($dy/dt$), and post-impact immobility.
  • DPDP Act 2023 On-Demand Privacy Radar: Video frames are strictly processed in volatile local RAM. Zero frames are ever written to disk or transmitted to external servers. Furthermore, the physical camera hardware is opened only on-demand when an authorized stream connects; closing the tab immediately releases cap.release() and physically extinguishes the hardware webcam LED.
  • Bidirectional In-Browser YOLO Frame Bridge: Added endpoint POST /api/yolo/process_frame in the local sentinel. When an evaluator accesses the portal via a standard browser camera (getUserMedia), the client pipes compressed snapshots to the local sentinel, receiving back 17 keypoint coordinates to project the high-precision green skeleton overlay directly onto the HTML5 video canvas.
  • Universal Client-Side Optical Differencing Fallback: If the local Python YOLO daemon is unreachable (e.g., when evaluated on external mobile devices or Macs without Python), the browser seamlessly falls back to a custom client-side luminance-differencing engine. It features sustained multi-frame descent corroboration ($\ge 3$ consecutive frames), human bounding box scaling, and floor-level gating ($Y_c > 52\%$), completely eliminating single-frame mouse-movement false alarms.
  • Continuous Keypoint Inference & Debounced Watchdog: Fixed transient polling disconnects by introducing a thread-safe inference lock (inference_lock = threading.Lock()), a 3-strike failure debounce counter, a 3000ms HTTP timeout tolerance, an inflight watchdog, and a 1.8-second keypoint decay buffer, ensuring uninterrupted pose rendering during rapid motion.
  • Synchronized Bed-Fall Clinical Demonstration: Included a high-definition 15-second hospital ward video asset (patient_bed_fall_demo.mp4) with dynamic source switching (POST /api/yolo/source?source=bed_fall_demo), allowing judges on any device to witness real-time YOLO pose tracking transition from in-bed supine sleep to floor collapse with one click.

C. Reasoning, Hypothesis Arbitration & Response Infrastructure

  • 9-Way Multimodal Physical Hypothesis Engine: When an anomaly occurs, the engine evaluates 9 competing physical explanations: Normal Activity, Intentional Sitting, Intentional Lying, Kneeling, Mechanical Trip, Loss of Balance, Acute Fall, Fall with Immobility, and Unknown.
  • Counterfactual Deductive Reasoning: Automatically generates plain-English medical justifications for rejecting benign alternatives (e.g., "Fall hypothesis supported: downward velocity $-1.84\text{ m/s}$ with torso angle $78.2^\circ$ at floor level; intentional sitting rejected due to absence of seated deceleration profile").
  • Interactive Resident Safety Verification Dialog (30s Grace Window): High-contrast emergency modal featuring a 30-second circular countdown timer, audio chime, and accessible action buttons ("I'm Okay", "I Fell (Minor)", "Need Help", "Device Drop").
  • Posture Equilibrium Auto-Recovery: If the resident regains upright standing posture within 5 seconds of an impact event, the alert automatically self-cancels, eliminating unnecessary caregiver panic.
  • Deterministic 3-Tier Emergency Call Ladder: The instant an unverified danger alert occurs, an automated call sequence begins: (1) Primary Caregiver → (2) Backup Contact (with 2 automatic retries) → (3) Emergency Services 108/112 (with automated GPS and clinical vitals payload).
  • Hands-Free Silent Background Boot: Configured tools/start_all_silent.vbs and registered a Windows Startup shortcut. Whenever the laptop powers on, both port 8080 (Web App) and port 5050 (YOLO Edge Sentinel) launch invisibly in the background.

7. Comprehensive List of Recommended Technical Fixes & Enhancements

To achieve complete production readiness and maximize competitive scoring at the IIT Bombay National Screening, the following engineering enhancements and technical fixes are recommended:

Fix 1 (P0 Priority): "Add Patient" Dynamic Registration Flow (Web & Mobile)

Current Limitation: The web prototype and mobile app currently bind data isolation to 4 pre-seeded clinical demo patients (Anita Sharma, Ram Prakash, Meera Nair, Kavitha Rao). When a new family caregiver registers an account via /api/auth/register, their roster defaults to 0 patients with no interactive UI mechanism to create a new patient profile.

Recommended Fix: 1. Implement POST /api/patients in prototype/server.js accepting Name, Age, Biological Sex, Chronic Condition (Hypertension, Diabetes, Cardiac, Post-Surgery, General Frailty), Island Address (e.g. Junglighat, Bambooflat, Hut Bay), and Room/Location.
2. Add an intuitive "Register New Family Member" modal on the React dashboard and Android app with instant baseline telemetry generation.
3. Persist user-created patients into data/patients.json with user ownership isolation.

Fix 2 (P0 Priority): Distributed Cloud Database Layer (PostgreSQL / Supabase / Neon)

Current Limitation: On serverless platforms like Vercel (rejivan2.vercel.app), the underlying execution instances are stateless and ephemeral. Any patient registrations, medication adherence marks ("Take Now"), or audit logs written to local JSON files are stored in-memory per container and do not survive instance recycling.

Recommended Fix: 1. Integrate a lightweight serverless cloud PostgreSQL database (such as Neon Postgres or Supabase) or Redis store (Upstash).
2. Provide environment variable fallback: if DATABASE_URL is present, query cloud SQL; otherwise, fall back gracefully to local JSON storage for zero-dependency offline laptop execution.

Fix 3 (P1 Priority): In-Browser MediaPipe Pose WebAssembly Integration

Current Limitation: While the local Python YOLO-Pose daemon functions flawlessly on developer machines with NVIDIA GPUs, remote evaluators visiting rejivan2.vercel.app on iOS, Android, or macOS without running the local Python script rely on optical pixel differencing, which lacks 17-point skeletal joint overlays.

Recommended Fix: 1. Bundle the compiled Google MediaPipe Pose WebAssembly / WebGL neural network directly inside the browser client bundle.
2. When the local YOLO daemon on port 5050 is unreachable, automatically initialize client-side MediaPipe Pose in-browser, delivering full 33-point 3D skeletal tracking directly on any device without requiring Python installation.

Fix 4 (P1 Priority): Physical Android Device BLE Hardware Validation

Current Limitation: The native Android application (app-android/) contains complete Kotlin data models and UI for medical devices, but currently consumes simulated telemetry packets over REST or local generation.

Recommended Fix: 1. Implement a native Android Bluetooth Low Energy GATT service (BluetoothGattCallback) scanning for standard Pulse Oximeter Profile (UUID 0x1822) and Heart Rate Service (UUID 0x180D).
2. Validate live physical wireless data streaming using a commercial finger pulse oximeter or smartwatch.

Fix 5 (P2 Priority): Full-Duplex WebRTC Voice Intercom Bridge

Current Limitation: The 30-second Resident Safety Verification dialog features audio chimes and visual countdowns, but family caregivers or ward nurses cannot speak directly to the resident through the camera sentinel.

Recommended Fix: 1. Add a WebRTC bidirectional audio channel between the web dashboard and the monitoring sentinel.
2. During an unverified impact event, the caregiver's microphone automatically connects, allowing them to speak reassuringly to the elderly resident: "We detected a sudden drop, are you alright?"

Fix 6 (P2 Priority): Multi-Bed Ward Matrix with Bedside Auto-Focus

Current Limitation: The Virtual Ward interface currently displays bed cards and a single focused camera feed. In a full hospital ward (e.g. GB Pant Hospital Ward A, 12 beds), nurses require an eagle-eye view of all beds simultaneously.

Recommended Fix: 1. Implement a 2x3 responsive CCTV matrix showing live low-framerate skeletal feeds for all ward beds.
2. If any bed triggers an elevated NEWS2 score or movement anomaly, automatically maximize and highlight that specific bed's stream on the nurse station console.
